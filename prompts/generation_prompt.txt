DEMOLY CORE IDENTITY & AUTHENTIC BACKSTORY:
================================================================================
Demoly is an AI-powered browser screen recorder, client handover platform, and visual bug reporting tool built specifically for tech agencies, web development studios, and QA teams.

1. THE FOUNDER ORIGIN STORY (WHY WE CREATED IT):
   Demoly was created by the co-founders of Creative Upaay, a digital agency that delivered web apps and AI-native products for over 85 global clients. While delivering a massive platform for an enterprise law firm client requiring 35–60 handover walkthrough videos, a severe operational bottleneck erupted:
   - Clients refused to watch 10-minute videos to find a 10-second instruction.
   - Clients repeatedly scheduled 15-30 minute calls to ask questions already answered in the videos.
   - Agency developers lost 2 to 4 billable hours per week answering repetitive queries.
   - Handover recordings could not remain passive, dead video files—they had to actively answer client questions.

2. HOW DEMOLY BEATS TRADITIONAL ALTERNATIVES:
   - LOOM: Only searches spoken audio transcripts. Silent button clicks = invisible to Loom. Also $20/seat/month.
   - GOOGLE DRIVE: Fails to stream files over ~100MB in-browser. Forces clients to download massive files.
   - DEMOLY'S BREAKTHROUGH: Captures every visual click, input, and navigation with timestamped precision.
   - INTERACTIVE PUBLIC LINKS: Clients "Talk to the Video" — AI jumps straight to the exact second the answer appears.
   - MCP INTEGRATION: Works as an MCP server for Cursor, Claude Code, Antigravity — feeds visual bug data to AI agents.
   - PRIVACY MASKING: Element-level masking that locks onto UI nodes — never just a static pixel blur.
