DEMOLY CORE IDENTITY & AUTHENTIC BACKSTORY:
================================================================================
Demoly is an AI-powered browser screen recorder, client handover platform, and visual bug reporting tool built specifically for tech agencies, web development studios, and QA teams.

1. THE FOUNDER ORIGIN STORY (WHY WE CREATED IT):
   Demoly was created by the co-founders of Creative Pie, a digital agency that delivered web apps and AI-native products for over 85 global clients. While delivering a massive platform for an enterprise law firm client requiring 35–60 handover walkthrough videos, a severe operational bottleneck erupted:
   - Clients and their team refused to watch 10-minute videos to find a 10-second instruction or button.
   - Clients repeatedly scheduled 15-30 minute Google Meet calls to ask questions about features ALREADY explained in the videos.
   - Agency developers lost 2 to 4 billable hours every single week per team member answering repetitive queries.
   - Handover recordings could not remain passive, dead video files—they had to actively answer client questions.

2. HOW DEMOLY IS BETTER (COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES):
   - LOOM LIMITATION: Loom only records pixels and searches spoken audio transcripts. If an action or UI button was clicked silently without speaking its exact name on microphone, Loom's AI is completely blind and cannot find it. Loom also charges $20/month per seat.
   - GOOGLE DRIVE LIMITATION: Google Drive fails to stream video files exceeding ~100MB directly in the browser, forcing clients to download massive files locally. Even with Gemini Drive integration, it cannot inspect on-screen visual DOM state.
   - DEMOLY'S BREAKTHROUGH: Demoly captures the browser Document Object Model (DOM) alongside video. Every click, input field, layout state, and route change is indexed with timestamped precision.
   - INTERACTIVE PUBLIC LINKS: Clients open a shared Demoly link and "Talk to the Video" using conversational AI. The AI searches the visual DOM and jumps straight to the exact second where the answer appears—no meetings needed.
   - DEVELOPER MCP INTEGRATION: Demoly integrates as a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for Cursor, Claude Code, and Antigravity, feeding visual bug recordings, DOM snapshots, network requests, and console errors directly to AI coding agents.
   - PRIVACY MASKING: Element-level DOM masking hides client PII, Stripe keys, and credentials non-destructively in the DOM layer (never rasterized or permanently blurred into pixels).
